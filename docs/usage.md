# Usage

This guide explains how to open a gallery, browse it, and download photos. You don't need any technical setup for this.

## Before you start

The gallery shows photos from a Google Drive folder. The folder owner needs to share it so the gallery can read it, usually as **Anyone with the link**. If the gallery shows no photos, check [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Get the folder link

1. Open the folder in Google Drive.
2. Click **Share** and make sure the folder is shared with anyone who has the link.
3. Click **Copy link**. The link should contain `/folders/` followed by a long ID.

## Open a gallery

1. Go to the gallery site.
2. Paste the folder link into the box.
3. Click **Generate**.

The gallery opens and the first photos appear. Use a Drive folder link, not a link to a single file.

## Browse photos

- Photos load in a grid. The grid adjusts to the width of your screen.
- More photos keep loading in batches while you scroll. You'll see a "Loading..." message while this happens. Loading takes a few seconds per batch, so a large folder may take a while to finish.
- Hover over a photo to see the whole picture fit inside its frame. Photos are cropped to fill the frame when you're not hovering.

## Open a photo

Click any photo to open a larger preview.

To close the preview, use any of these:

- Press the **Escape** key.
- Press your browser's **Back** button. On a phone, use the device's back gesture.
- Click the dark area outside the preview. The preview has no close button.

## Download a photo

1. Open the photo in the preview.
2. Click **Download** below the photo.

Your browser saves the image as a file.

## Open the folder in Google Drive

Use the **Go to Drive** button at the top right of the gallery to open the original folder in Google Drive.
